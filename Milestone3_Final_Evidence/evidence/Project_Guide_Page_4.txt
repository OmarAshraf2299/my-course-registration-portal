Software Maintenance | Six Biweekly Milestones | Student Version 
Milestone 3 — Corrective Maintenance 
Due: End of Week 6   |   Goal: Fix documented defects without breaking existing behavior.  
Required work 
• Reproduce and document at least three pre-existing defects. 
• For each defect: record steps to reproduce, root cause, changed files and expected behavior.  
• Implement and commit fixes in traceable increments. 
• Execute focused tests and a basic regression check for affected features.  
• Update maintenance log and attach before/after evidence.  
What to submit 
• Working corrected software and commits linked to defect IDs.  
• Three defect reports with before/after evidence. 
• Test results and updated maintenance log.
